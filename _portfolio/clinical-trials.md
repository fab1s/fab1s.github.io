## Project focus

The project began with a professor’s hunch about how race and ethnicity are reported in clinical trials. I was asked to assess whether data from ClinicalTrials.gov could support an investigation of the question.

## My contribution

I worked with a large, relational ClinicalTrials.gov dataset in R, building a pipeline to extract, link, clean, and filter the relevant trial records. I produced descriptive statistics and a transparent sample-selection workflow.

## Research outputs

The data work supported a [published article](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/09581596.2026.2670065#abstract) and a submitted manuscript, *How U.S. Race and Ethnicity Categories Travel: Clinical Trials, Global Infrastructures, and National Classification Cultures in Europe*.
